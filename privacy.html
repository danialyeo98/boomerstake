Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

BoomerStake Group ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact details you give us: when you use our contact form or email us, we receive your name, email address and the contents of your message.
  • Usage data: basic, non-identifying analytics such as pages visited, device type and referring site, used to improve the Site.
  • Cookies: small files that help the Site function and let us understand aggregate usage.

We do not collect payment information — we never process deposits, withdrawals or wagers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide support.
  • To operate, maintain and improve the Site and our content.
  • To understand which information and partners are most useful to our community.

3. Cookies & Analytics

We may use cookies and third-party analytics to measure traffic and improve the experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; some features may not work as intended if you do.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your contact details with Partners. When you click through to a Partner, you do so directly — any information you then provide is governed by that Partner's own privacy policy. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work to safeguard the limited data we hold.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you. To make a request, email boomergroup@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Third-Party Links

Our Site links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices and encourage you to read their policies before sharing any information.

8. Children

Our Site and the platforms we refer to are strictly for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest revision.

10. Contact

For any privacy question or request, contact us at boomergroup@gmail.com or via Telegram.
